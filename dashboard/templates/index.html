RECOVERY SEQUENCE — FUNCTIONAL INTENT

1
Fault detection

Watchdog detects a missed heartbeat or timeout condition.

2
Fault classification

Fault manager records the fault and assigns its severity.

3
Processor reset

Recovery controller requests a controlled processor reset.

4
Heartbeat confirmation

Controller checks for processor activity after reset release.

5
Recovery outcome

Controller reports success or failure after its retry policy.

This describes the intended recovery flow. Do not interpret it as proof that the complete top-level sequence is already working.
